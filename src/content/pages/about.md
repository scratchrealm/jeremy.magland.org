# About

Jeremy Magland is a Data Scientist in the
[Center for Computational Mathematics](https://www.simonsfoundation.org/flatiron/center-for-computational-mathematics)
at the [Flatiron Institute](https://www.simonsfoundation.org/flatiron), which
he joined in 2015. His work focuses on developing computational methods and
open-source software for analyzing, visualizing, and sharing large-scale
scientific data.

He has developed algorithms for clustering and spike sorting — including the
[MountainSort](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2017.08.030) spike sorter —
and contributed to community tools for electrophysiology such as SpikeForest
and SpikeInterface. His current work concentrates on reproducible,
cloud-friendly tools that make complex datasets easier to explore and reuse,
including [Neurosift](https://neurosift.app/) for browser-based exploration of
the DANDI Archive and other neurophysiology repositories and
[figpack](https://flatironinstitute.github.io/figpack/) for portable
interactive visualizations. Recent projects include
[numbl](https://numbl.org), a MATLAB-compatible computing environment that
runs in the browser and on the command line, and [mip](https://mip.sh), a
package manager for MATLAB. He has also explored applications of large
language models to scientific data analysis, including
[assistants and automated notebook generators](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-06285-x)
for the DANDI Archive.

Before joining the foundation, Magland was a research associate professor of
radiology at the University of Pennsylvania, where he worked on mathematical
modeling, image reconstruction, and data processing for magnetic resonance
imaging, including pulse sequence design and quantitative imaging of bone and
vasculature.

He holds a B.S. and M.S. in mathematics from Brigham Young University and a
Ph.D. in mathematics from the University of Pennsylvania.
